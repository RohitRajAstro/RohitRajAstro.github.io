#### Teaching

**Juniata College — TA & Head Evening Group Tutor** (2022–2025)
Intro Physics I & II, Modern Physics, and Electromagnetism. I ran evening group tutoring designed around how each student actually learned rather than how the textbook was ordered — the sessions grew into some of the most attended teaching-support hours on campus.

**UCSC Science Internship Program — Research Mentor** (2024)
Mentored three high-school interns through their first real research projects on Wolf-Rayet stars, from Python basics to presenting at regional conferences. To build trust, I learned greetings in each intern's native language — small things matter when you're teaching someone to believe they belong in science.

#### Outreach

**Cambridge, MA** (2025–present) — Volunteer with *Cambridge Explores the Universe* (running the MicroObservatory station and serving as an Ask-an-Astronomer representative) and the *Cambridge Science Festival*.

**Multilingual science communication** — With UCSC's *Shadow the Scientist* program, I'm developing a source-grounded AI workflow that turns technical astronomy into podcasts in 70+ languages, presented at STScI's *Language AI in the Space Sciences* workshop (2026). Earlier, I helped coordinate an "Astronomy Night at W.M. Keck Observatory" event for students in a terrorism-affected region of Kashmir, India.

**SPS & Sigma Pi Sigma National Council** (2024–2025) — Led undergraduate engagement efforts across the Northeast US, organizing regional physics meetings and advising the AIP Board on student involvement.

#### The learning center

In 2020, I founded a free learning center in Begusarai, Bihar — the region where I grew up without labs, computers, or reliable teachers. It now serves about 50 students with three teachers, and I'm integrating AI-generated regional-language STEM materials so the next kid with scavenged textbooks has a shorter road than I did. It remains the project I am proudest of.
