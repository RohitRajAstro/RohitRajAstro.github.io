Research Experience

  • Sep 2025 – Present
    SPARK Postbac Fellow
    Center for Astrophysics | Harvard & Smithsonian, Cambridge, MA
    • Modeling the surface mass ejection driving Betelgeuse's "Great Dimming" with the Rybicki–Hummer non-LTE radiative transfer code; reproduced observed Mg II chromospheric line asymmetries and quantified ejection velocity and mass-loss rate, consistent with HST/STIS UV time-domain spectra. First-author paper in preparation.
    • Building a neural spectral emulator to forward-model the effect of magnetic spot distributions on cool-star spectra, with applications to next-generation high-resolution spectrographs.
    • Advisors: Dr. Andrea Dupree, Dr. Paul Cristofari
  • Jun – Aug 2024
    SIP Research Lead
    University of California, Santa Cruz
    • Led spectroscopic and photometric analysis of Wolf-Rayet and unusual emission-line stars in M31, M32, and M33 using Keck/DEIMOS and HST PHATTER/PHAST photometry; mentored three high-school interns.
    • Developed a 2D Gaussian crowding-analysis pipeline; recovered sub-arcsecond (<0.1″) coordinates for >90% of the M33 WR sample and showed that blending in fiber-scale surveys systematically biases WR subtype classifications and mass-loss estimates. ApJ paper in review; Keck OSIRIS LGSAO follow-up awarded (2026B, Program U221).
    • Advisors: Prof. Raja GuhaThakurta, Dr. Robert Williams
  • Jun – Aug 2023
    SIP Research Lead
    University of California, Santa Cruz
    • Identified systematic velocity suppression in DESI fiber spectroscopy relative to DEIMOS slit spectroscopy in M31's crowded disk, revealing precision limits of 1.5″-fiber spectrographs for resolving individual stellar kinematics. Presented at the 243rd AAS.
    • Developed an HST-based crowding-contamination metric ranking blending severity across survey targets.
    • Advisor: Prof. Raja GuhaThakurta
  • Aug – Dec 2024
    Theoretical Physics Research Student
    Imperial College London (remote)
    • Developed FDTD simulations in Python for EM wave propagation in media with time-varying permittivity, including optical cloaking in quasi-crystals; reproduced results from seminal literature.
    • Advisor: Dr. T. V. Raziman
  • May – Aug 2023
    SOAR Scholar
    Emory University, Laney Graduate School (remote)
    • Theoretical reading project on classical and quantum wave equations; applications of Noether's theorem to the classical and Schrödinger wave equations.
  • May – Aug 2022
    Research Intern
    Case Western Reserve University, Cleveland, OH
    • Implemented matched filtering and Fourier analysis to reproduce LIGO's GW150914 binary black hole detection; developed educational materials on gravitational-wave data analysis.
    • Mentor: Prof. Harsh Mathur
