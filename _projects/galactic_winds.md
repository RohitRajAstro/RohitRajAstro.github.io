Every project on this page is secretly about the same thing: mass leaving stars. Wolf-Rayet winds strip entire solar masses; Betelgeuse coughed off a continent-sized parcel of chromosphere while we watched. The natural next question is — *where does it all go?*

The answer, at galactic scale, is **winds**: star formation and stellar death collectively drive gas and dust out of galaxies, regulating how they grow, enriching the space between them, and setting the chemistry of the next stellar generation. It is one of the least constrained ingredients in galaxy evolution.
